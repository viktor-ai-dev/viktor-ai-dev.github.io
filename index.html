Data Warehouse Architecture

The warehouse is built using a layered dbt architecture that separates data preparation, analytical modeling and business-facing reporting. This structure improves maintainability, data lineage and reusability across analytical workloads.

Staging Layer
Raw source data is standardized, cleaned and type-safe before entering the analytical warehouse.

stg_customers • stg_geolocation • stg_order_items • stg_order_payments • stg_order_reviews • stg_orders • stg_product_category_name_translation • stg_products • stg_sellers

Dimensional Layer
Reusable dimensions provide descriptive business context for analytical queries and reporting models.

dim_customers • dim_date • dim_payment_type • dim_products • dim_sellers

Fact Layer
Fact models capture transactional and event-level business activity for revenue, orders, payments and customer analysis.

fct_order_items • fct_orders • fct_payments • fct_reviews

Reporting Layer
Business-oriented reporting models transform the warehouse into ready-to-analyze datasets for dashboards and KPI reporting.

rpt_customer_metrics • rpt_customer_retention • rpt_product_performance • rpt_sales_by_category • rpt_sales_daily
