Business Problem

Raw e-commerce transaction data is difficult to analyze directly and is distributed across multiple source datasets. The platform creates a reliable analytical foundation for revenue, product, customer and retention intelligence.

End-to-End Pipeline

01 · Ingestion Raw CSV datasets are converted into Parquet for efficient analytical storage.
02 · Cloud Storage Parquet datasets are uploaded to Google Cloud Storage.
03 · Warehouse Ingestion BigQuery External Tables provide warehouse access to the cloud-hosted datasets.
04 · Transformation dbt builds the analytical warehouse through staging, dimensional, fact and reporting models.
05 · Orchestration Kestra coordinates dependent workflows across the data pipeline.
06 · Analytics Business-ready reporting models are consumed by an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Analytical Design

The dimensional model separates measurable business events from reusable business dimensions. Reporting models then expose stable metrics without requiring the dashboard to perform complex warehouse joins.

Data Quality

dbt tests validate uniqueness, required fields and relationships across the analytical model. Business-level reconciliation validates metric consistency from facts through the reporting layer.

Architecture principle: Raw source data is transformed into reusable analytical models once, while business reporting logic remains separated from the presentation layer.

Data Quality & Validation

Data quality is treated as a first-class part of the platform rather than as a final validation step.

Schema Integrity

Uniqueness and not-null tests protect critical identifiers and required analytical fields.

Referential Integrity

Relationship tests validate fact-to-dimension mappings across the warehouse.

Business Reconciliation

Revenue and order metrics are reconciled between the underlying facts and downstream reporting models to ensure aggregation consistency.

Business Intelligence

The reporting layer feeds an interactive Streamlit dashboard designed for executive-level visibility and exploratory business analysis.

Pipeline Orchestration

Kestra coordinates the pipeline as a sequence of dependent workflows, separating ingestion, cloud storage, warehouse creation and dbt transformation.

Current workflow:

csv_to_parquet → upload_parquet → create_ext_tables → dbt_create_stg_tables → dbt_create_marts

The final marts workflow builds the dimensional, fact and reporting layers consumed by the dashboard.

Key Engineering Decisions

Parquet

Columnar storage format selected for analytical efficiency and compact cloud storage.

BigQuery

Serverless analytical warehouse providing scalable SQL analytics and native integration with GCS.

dbt

Version-controlled, modular and testable transformation layer with explicit model dependencies.

Kestra

Workflow orchestration layer responsible for execution order and cross-stage dependencies.

Project Outcomes

The platform produces a complete analytical data environment rather than a collection of isolated scripts.
