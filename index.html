Data Warehouse Architecture

The warehouse follows a layered dbt architecture with a dedicated staging layer, dimensional marts and a business-oriented reporting layer.

Staging:
stg_customers • stg_geolocation • stg_order_items • stg_order_payments • stg_order_reviews • stg_orders • stg_product_category_name_translation • stg_products • stg_sellers

Dimensions:
dim_customers • dim_date • dim_payment_type • dim_products • dim_sellers

Facts:
fct_order_items • fct_orders • fct_payments • fct_reviews

Reporting:
rpt_customer_metrics • rpt_customer_retention • rpt_product_performance • rpt_sales_by_category • rpt_sales_daily

Data Quality & Validation

dbt tests are applied across the analytical models to validate uniqueness, non-null constraints and referential integrity.

Business-level reconciliation was also used to verify that revenue and order metrics remain consistent from fact tables through the reporting layer.

Business Intelligence

The reporting layer feeds an interactive Streamlit dashboard covering revenue trends, category performance, top products, customer metrics, repeat purchase behavior and retention analysis.

Pipeline Orchestration

Kestra orchestrates the complete workflow through separate pipeline stages:

csv_to_parquet → upload_parquet → create_ext_tables → dbt_create_stg_tables → dbt_create_marts
